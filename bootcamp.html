Sound familiar?

Wanting to build is clear. Knowing what to build is not.

If any of this describes you, nothing is wrong with you. These are common places to get stuck during a transition.

  • You want to build something meaningful, and you do not know what.
  • You have knowledge and experience, and you overlook how much they are worth.
  • You are not sure who you could help.
  • You feel pressure to start over or to find something extraordinary.
  • You have ideas, and you cannot see a practical next step.
The shift

Building can begin with what you already carry.

Starting over is not the only way forward. You can start by recognising your raw material, connecting it to a real need, and taking action.

Step 1
Recognise what you carry
Your knowledge, skills, experience, strengths and perspective are raw material.
Step 2
Connect it to a real need
Link what you carry to the people you could help, a problem they face, and the change you could help create.
Step 3
Take action
Choose a practical next step and begin moving.
What you walk away with

By the end, you will have worked towards:

The Bootcamp is a guided starting point. It does not promise a complete business idea, a finished offer, customers or income.

  • A clearer understanding of the strengths, knowledge, skills and experiences you can draw upon.
  • A better understanding of the people you may be able to help.
  • A clearer connection between your gift, a problem and a possible transformation.
  • A starting point for exploring what you could build.
  • Practical next steps you can begin acting on.
The three-day experience

Three connected stages. Each one builds on the last.

Day 1
DISCOVER

Day 3

Who should attend

You do not need to have it all figured out.

Arriving with questions is fine. The Bootcamp is built for exactly that.

  • You are a new or aspiring entrepreneur.
  • You are navigating a career, business or personal transition and want to explore what you could build next.
  • You have knowledge, skills or experience, and you do not know how to turn them into something useful for others.
  • You are ready to move beyond thinking and begin taking practical steps.
